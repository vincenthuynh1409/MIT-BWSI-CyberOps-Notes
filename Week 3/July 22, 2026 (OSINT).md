# July 22, 2026 (OSINT)

**Open Source Intelligence (OSINT)** = basic discipline of intelligence collection derived from publicly available data sources and information

<u>Key Notes:</u>
- **Before**
	- Authorization
	- Requirements
	- Reason
- **During**
	- OPSEC
	- Sanitation
	- Document evidence
	- Contingency procedures
- **After**
	- Note-taking
	- Verify data
	- Sanitation

<u>OPSEC (Operational Security):</u>
- Antithesis to OSINT
- OPSEC revolves around denying adversaries ability to collect (CALI):
	- **Capabilities**
	- **Activities**
	- **Limitations**
	- **Intentions**

<u>Data Brokers - Benefits:</u>
- **New Datapoints**
	- Adds datapoints to ID understand activity or subjects of interest
- **Supported Analyses**
	- Link, ex: family trees
	- Temporal, ex: change of address
- **Research Workload:**
	- some data collection

<u>Data Brokers - Drawbacks:</u>
- **Accuracy** (stale and mismatched data)
- **Law** (poor civil protections, and weak privacy laws)
- **Privacy** (ethically questionable, and can lead to personal threats)
- **Opt-Out** (complex time consuming process)

<u>OSINT Types:</u>
- **Passive:**
	- **Indirect Observation:**
		- Third party research
		- Google dorks, Maps
		- WHOIS
		- Shodan
		- Public records
		- Cached webpage, wayback
- **Active:**
	- **Direct Observation:**
		- Web-crawling
		- Nmap
		- Ping
		- Emailing
