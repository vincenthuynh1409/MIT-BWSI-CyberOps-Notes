# July 22, 2026 (LLMs)

<u>Transformers:</u>
- A **transformer** is a neural network design that powers **large language models (LLMs)** through three main parts: self-attention mechanisms, parallel processing, and stacked layers.

<u>Attention:</u>
- **LLM attention** is a core machine learning mechanism that allows a model to weigh the importance of different words (tokens) in a sequence and understand context. It relies on three primary components: **Queries**, **Keys**, and **Values**
